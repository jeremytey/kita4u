# Kita4U: pitch claims

What the deck and the presenter may say about the prototype, and what they may not. Checked against the app on 2026-10-19 (phase 12E).

Demo data demonstrates what the software could do. Evidence claims describe what actually happened. Only the first is allowed until interviews exist.

## Safe to say
- Kita4U is an interactive prototype with simulated data.
- An opt-in, trusted Circle: three people around one resident (a neighbour, a family member and a proposed community role).
- Consent is shown for one contact (Mei): she can accept or decline the invitation. It is a prototype of consent; it does not change the chain.
- Reminders are simulated.
- The escalation order is fixed in the prototype (a demo setting): neighbour, family, then the proposed community role.
- Timings are demo settings. A pilot would agree them with each Circle.
- If a contact says they will check and records no outcome within 1 hour, the next contact is asked.
- Township Support is a proposed role, to be agreed in a pilot.
- 999 is outside the chain. Kita4U does not replace emergency services.
- Connect is a proposed expansion, shown as a labelled preview.

## Do not say
- That the app is working, validated or proven.
- "Residents want", "residents agreed", "residents joined".
- Any count or percentage of residents, sign-ups or participation.
- "Chosen contact sequence" (the order is fixed).
- "Authorised contact".
- "Automated reminders" (they are simulated).
- "Seen", "On the way" or "Reached" as features.
- That SDP guardhouses already do welfare checks.
- That sports or activities work in the prototype.
- KitaCare or KitaConnect as separate products.

## Deck wording fixes
- "agreed order (fixed in the prototype)"
- "Circle member", not "authorised contact"
- "reminders are simulated"
- "interactive prototype with simulated data"
- "no outcome within 1 hour, the next contact is asked"
- "timings are demo settings"
